# Talk station scripts

Words approved by the Mabou main thread on 15 Nov 2026, with the changes it asked for. These are the texts the voices read.

- **Station:** 94.9 THE PARTY LINE. It plays on both games and in an 1884 car, like every other station.
- **Shows:** *Mail Madness* (call-in) and *Just Drive: the developer* (interview).
- **Host of both:** Effie. She is fed up with the callers and defends the carrier.
- **Every name is invented.** Callers have first names only. "The developer" is a fictional character. He has no name and a stock voice.
- **The carrier** is the player. He speaks only as "a concerned citizen": in the last call of round 1, and in the quiz of round 2.
- Each segment is one track. The radio can play them in any order, so each one stands alone.
- `[SFX: ...]` is a sound effect. `[CLIP: ...]` is a few seconds of a song that is already on the radio.
- The heading of each segment is its proposed file name.

## Part 1: Mail Madness (call-in)

### talk-call-wrong-box

**EFFIE:** This is Mail Madness on the Party Line, the only show where the mail is late and the callers are early. I'm Effie. Line one, Theresa in Mabou. Go ahead, Theresa.

**THERESA:** Effie, I got the neighbour's mail again.

**EFFIE:** Again.

**THERESA:** Fourth time this month. I have his power bill, his seed catalogue and a postcard from his sister.

**EFFIE:** And you walked it over to him.

**THERESA:** Well. Eventually.

**EFFIE:** Theresa.

**THERESA:** I didn't open anything! A postcard is facing up. That's not opening. That's just reading.

**EFFIE:** And what did the sister say?

**THERESA:** She's not coming for the holidays. And Effie, I think it's about the casserole.

**EFFIE:** So the carrier put it in the wrong box.

**THERESA:** He didn't put it anywhere. He threw it. Out the window. Moving.

**EFFIE:** He throws from the window. That's the job. The boxes are on the right, the wheel is on the right, and the man is doing geometry at forty kilometres an hour.

**THERESA:** My box and the neighbour's box are a hundred metres apart!

**EFFIE:** And he hit one of them. Where I come from, that's fifty percent.

**THERESA:** You always take his side.

**EFFIE:** Somebody has to. He's out there alone with a car full of flyers and a dog behind him. What do you want done about it, Theresa?

**THERESA:** I want my own mail.

**EFFIE:** And who has your mail?

**THERESA:** The neighbour, I suppose.

**EFFIE:** Has he read it?

**THERESA:** He waved at me funny on Tuesday.

**EFFIE:** Then you're even. Bring the man his power bill, get your own mail back, and for the love of mercy, sort out the casserole. Line two, you're on Mail Madness.

### talk-call-hedge

**EFFIE:** Back on Mail Madness. Angus is calling from Port Hood. Angus, what did he do?

**ANGUS:** He went through my hedge, Effie.

**EFFIE:** Through it.

**ANGUS:** In one side and out the other. Grey car. Wheel on the wrong side. I was standing right there with the clippers.

**EFFIE:** Angus, I have to stop you. The carrier doesn't deliver in Port Hood.

**ANGUS:** I know he doesn't!

**EFFIE:** So what was he doing in your hedge?

**ANGUS:** That's what I asked him! He rolled the window down and said he was just driving.

**EFFIE:** Just driving.

**ANGUS:** "Just driving." Like it's a thing a person does. No mail. No parcel. He came all the way from Mabou to not deliver anything to me, through a hedge.

**EFFIE:** How's the hedge?

**ANGUS:** It has a door in it now.

**EFFIE:** Is it a good door?

**ANGUS:** It's car-shaped, Effie. The wife says it frames the water nicely. That is not the point.

**EFFIE:** Did he say anything else?

**ANGUS:** He said there were cows on the road and he had a photograph.

**EFFIE:** Well, there you go. He had a photograph.

**ANGUS:** Of cows! Not of my hedge!

**EFFIE:** Angus, the man keeps records. Cows on the road, he goes around. Your hedge was around. That is not carelessness. That is a detour with paperwork.

**ANGUS:** Twenty-two years I grew that hedge.

**EFFIE:** And it stopped him for nearly a second. Be proud of it. Here's what you do. Plant something in the gap. Something sturdy.

**ANGUS:** Like what?

**EFFIE:** A mailbox. He can't hit those either. Thanks for the call, Angus.

### talk-call-washboard

**EFFIE:** Mail Madness. Hughie is on line two. Hughie drives the Mabou Mines Road every day. Hughie, how's the road?

**HUGHIE:** Washboard, Effie.

**EFFIE:** Still?

**HUGHIE:** It was washboard when my grandfather was a boy. It'll be washboard when the sun burns out. I'm not calling about the road. I'm calling about what it does to my mail. It all comes over that road before it gets to me.

**EFFIE:** Go on.

**HUGHIE:** He comes over it too fast. Everything in that car gets shook. My magazine came with the pages in a different order.

**EFFIE:** That can't happen, Hughie.

**HUGHIE:** I read the ending first! And I ordered a jigsaw puzzle. A thousand pieces. A lighthouse.

**EFFIE:** And it came in pieces.

**HUGHIE:** It came done, Effie.

**EFFIE:** It came done.

**HUGHIE:** All that washboard at that speed, and the thing assembled itself in the box. All but the sky. What am I supposed to do in February now?

**EFFIE:** I'll be honest, Hughie, that sounds like a service.

**HUGHIE:** And the eggs from my sister.

**EFFIE:** Nobody should mail eggs.

**HUGHIE:** They arrived scrambled. In the shell. I don't know how you'd even do that on purpose.

**EFFIE:** So you want the road graded.

**HUGHIE:** Don't you dare!

**EFFIE:** No?

**HUGHIE:** That washboard is the only thing between the rest of us and him at full speed. You grade that road and he'll be through there like a rumour. Leave the road alone. I want him to slow down.

**EFFIE:** I'll tell him. He'll say his front shock is knocking and the garage will hear about it.

**HUGHIE:** The garage can hear it from here.

**EFFIE:** The road stays washboard, the carrier stays the carrier, and Hughie, do the sky. It'll take you to March. Line three.

### talk-call-wrong-side

**EFFIE:** At the sky, dear. Nobody can read that. You'll have your privacy at last. We'll be right back.

## Part 2: Just Drive, the developer (interview)

### talk-interview-land

**EFFIE:** This is the Party Line, and I'm Effie. Today there are no callers. I can't tell you how good that feels. I have a guest instead. He made a game called Just Drive. He asked me not to use his name, so I'll call him what everybody calls him: the developer. Welcome.

**DEVELOPER:** Thanks, Effie. Happy to be anywhere.

**EFFIE:** Just Drive. What do you do in it?

**DEVELOPER:** You drive.

**EFFIE:** And then?

**DEVELOPER:** No, that's it. It's in the name. We tested longer names. "Just Drive and Also Look at Things." It didn't fit on the card.

**EFFIE:** Where do you drive?

**DEVELOPER:** Cape Breton. The real land. Mabou, Port Hood, Judique, West Bay, Port Hawkesbury and Point Tupper, the Causeway and Troy. Every hill has its real shape.

**EFFIE:** You made all those hills?

**DEVELOPER:** No, the hills were here already. People write to me to say a hill is too steep. I tell them all the same thing. Take it up with the last ice age. I only copied the homework.

**EFFIE:** And the roads?

**DEVELOPER:** Real roads, real names. If the road has a bend in it, the game has the bend.

**EFFIE:** Is my house in it?

**DEVELOPER:** A shape of it, maybe. The buildings are shapes. The right size, in the right spot. No numbers on them, no names, and nobody inside.

**EFFIE:** Nobody inside.

**DEVELOPER:** It's a very quiet island in there. Nobody is home anywhere. It's the best traffic you'll ever have.

**EFFIE:** So I can't find myself in it.

**DEVELOPER:** You can find your road. You can find your hill. You can't find your kitchen. I think that's the right amount.

**EFFIE:** And when you get something wrong?

**DEVELOPER:** Somebody writes and tells me. The address is on the title card. One man wrote about a culvert. Four paragraphs. He was right about the culvert.

**EFFIE:** More with the developer after this.

### talk-interview-fleet

**EFFIE:** I'm back with the developer of Just Drive. Let's talk about the cars. What do I get?

**DEVELOPER:** You get the mail car. Grey, sensible, wheel on the right.

**EFFIE:** Why is the wheel on the right?

**DEVELOPER:** The mailboxes are on the right. You can't deliver across a passenger. People have tried. It ends in a ditch.

**EFFIE:** What kind of car is it?

**DEVELOPER:** A grey one.

**EFFIE:** But what make?

**DEVELOPER:** Grey. I'm not allowed to say more, and honestly, I've forgotten. It's the car your aunt would trust.

**EFFIE:** What else is in the garage?

**DEVELOPER:** A dirt bike, for people who think a road is only a suggestion. And if you know how to ask, a side by side and a heavy pickup.

**EFFIE:** And the horse.

**DEVELOPER:** The horse is for eighteen eighty-four. We'll come to that. The horse has no radio, which the horse prefers.

**EFFIE:** Can I break the car?

**DEVELOPER:** In Mail Madness, you can upset it. Hit enough things and it starts to tell you. A window. The alignment. A front shock starts knocking.

**EFFIE:** And then what?

**DEVELOPER:** Then you keep driving, with a knock. It's a very honest game. It's the same as owning a car.

**EFFIE:** Is there a horn?

**DEVELOPER:** There is a horn. I spent a long time on the horn.

**EFFIE:** What does it do?

**DEVELOPER:** Nothing. It's a horn. Things hear it and carry on. There's a cat. The cat is not impressed. The instructions say one word about it. Horn. So nobody gets their hopes up.

**EFFIE:** So I have five vehicles and a horn that does nothing.

**DEVELOPER:** And the whole west side of an island. I'd call that a full tank. More after the break, Effie?

**EFFIE:** I say when the break is. More after the break.

### talk-interview-1884

**EFFIE:** The developer is still here. I'm told your game has time travel in it.

**DEVELOPER:** A little. There's a door on the title card. It says, "Go back to Mabou in 1884." You press it, and you're there.

**EFFIE:** Just like that.

**DEVELOPER:** Just like that. I wanted to add lightning and a big machine, but a door is cheaper.

**EFFIE:** And what's there?

**DEVELOPER:** The same land. The hills don't change much in a hundred and forty years. But the roads are cart roads, narrow ones. The bridges are timber. And you're on a horse.

**EFFIE:** Is it real?

**DEVELOPER:** It's a story, built on a survey sheet from 1884. The places are close, not exact, and it doesn't show any person's land. I say so right on the card. I'd rather be honest than be sued by a great-grandmother.

**EFFIE:** Only Mabou?

**DEVELOPER:** Mabou and Judique, so far.

**EFFIE:** Is there mail in 1884?

**DEVELOPER:** No mail. No mailboxes. Nothing to hit.

**EFFIE:** He'll hate that.

**DEVELOPER:** He's very calm there, I'm told.

**EFFIE:** And there's no radio.

**DEVELOPER:** Not on the horse. A horse has no dashboard. So anybody listening to us on a horse should speak to someone.

**EFFIE:** How fast is the horse?

**DEVELOPER:** Horse speed. People ask for a faster horse. I tell them that's called a dirt bike. It's on the same card.

**EFFIE:** How do you get home?

**DEVELOPER:** There's another door. "Back to today." I wish I had one of those for Mondays.

**EFFIE:** What did you learn, building 1884?

**DEVELOPER:** That people then went a very long way round to avoid one hill. And that I'd have done exactly the same.

**EFFIE:** We'll be back. In this century.

### talk-interview-quarry-phones

**EFFIE:** Last part with the developer of Just Drive. Tell me about the quarry.

**DEVELOPER:** In West Bay there's an old gypsum quarry. It's real. It's been idle since about 2011, and it has two pit lakes in it.

**EFFIE:** And you put a park in it.

**DEVELOPER:** In the game, I did. Sugar Camp Off-Road Park. I made that up. The quarry is real and it's private land. So drive it in the game, and nowhere else. That's the whole point of the game. You can go, and nobody has to come and get you.

**EFFIE:** What's in the park?

**DEVELOPER:** A park map, two lookoffs, and signs. I like the signs. "Helmets on." "Easy lines are green." "Stay off the water."

**EFFIE:** Do people stay off the water?

**DEVELOPER:** Effie, nobody has ever stayed off the water. I put the sign up so I could say I told them.

**EFFIE:** Now, I heard this runs on a phone.

**DEVELOPER:** It does. Left thumb steers. There's a button that says GO and one that says BRAKE. I didn't want to confuse anybody.

**EFFIE:** Which do people press?

**DEVELOPER:** GO. I may as well not have drawn the other one.

**EFFIE:** Do I need the internet?

**DEVELOPER:** For most of it, yes. But you can download Mabou and play it with no connection. Which is handy, because that's about how much connection there is in parts of Mabou.

**EFFIE:** Can I drive with other people?

**DEVELOPER:** You can. An open road, or a private room with your friends. Everybody gets a made-up call sign. You can even race. Or you can all stop on a hill and look at the water. That's allowed too.

**EFFIE:** What's next for Just Drive?

**DEVELOPER:** More road.

**EFFIE:** And after that?

**DEVELOPER:** I'd have to drive there and see.

**EFFIE:** The developer, everybody. He's leaving now. On the right-hand side.

## Part 10: Just Drive, the developer comes back (interview)

### talk-interview-traffic

**EFFIE:** The developer of Just Drive is back in the studio. He asked to come back. I didn't ask him. What's new?

**DEVELOPER:** Traffic, Effie. I have given the highways traffic.

**EFFIE:** You say that like you invented it.

**DEVELOPER:** In a sense, I did. Until now, there was not one other car moving on that whole island. I looked at the empty road and I thought, this needs society.

**EFFIE:** How much society?

**DEVELOPER:** Four cars.

**EFFIE:** Four.

**DEVELOPER:** Blue, red, silver and black. On a slower machine, two. Anybody can make a traffic jam. It takes an artist to make four cars feel like a Tuesday.

**EFFIE:** Where do they drive?

**DEVELOPER:** On the highways. They keep to their lane, at a sensible speed. If you stop in front of one, it waits. Then, when the other lane is clear, it eases out and goes around you.

**EFFIE:** That's very polite.

**DEVELOPER:** It's the most polite driver in Nova Scotia, and I had to build it myself. They don't honk. They don't shake a fist. I considered it. I decided the world had enough.

**EFFIE:** And on the back roads?

**DEVELOPER:** Nobody. A back road should be yours. That's not a missing feature, Effie. That's a philosophy.

**EFFIE:** Where are the four cars going?

**DEVELOPER:** People ask me that. They are going to the end of the road, and then they are coming back. Which, if we're honest, is all any of us are doing.

**EFFIE:** More from this man after the break. I can't stop him.

### talk-interview-races

**EFFIE:** I'm still here with the developer of Just Drive. You've added racing. I thought the idea was that nobody's in a hurry.

**DEVELOPER:** It was. Then I let people drive together, and within a minute somebody said, "Race you to the bridge." I don't make human nature, Effie. I only host it.

**EFFIE:** How does it work?

**DEVELOPER:** You choose "Drive with others." The first driver in the room is the host. The host opens "Room race" and picks a short road course. A real road. A few hundred metres of it.

**EFFIE:** And then?

**DEVELOPER:** A gold ring appears at the start. Everybody drives to it and stops inside. I'd like to dwell on that. Stops. It is the only race I know of that begins with everyone parked.

**EFFIE:** Then a countdown.

**DEVELOPER:** Five seconds. Leave early, and you're out. After that you take the gold checkpoints in order, and the clock decides.

**EFFIE:** What do you win?

**DEVELOPER:** Your time, on a list, beside a made-up call sign. No trophy. No coins. No hat for the car. Somebody suggested hats. I left the room.

**EFFIE:** Whose room?

**DEVELOPER:** My own. I was alone. It was still the right thing to do.

**EFFIE:** And if nobody finishes?

**DEVELOPER:** It ends by itself after ten minutes. I find that true of most arguments. Shall we go on?

**EFFIE:** We'll take a break. That was not a question.

### talk-interview-beyond

**EFFIE:** Last part with the developer. People think this game is Mabou. It's not only Mabou now, is it?

**DEVELOPER:** Mabou was the first movement, Effie. There are six now. Judique. Port Hood. West Bay. Point Tupper and Port Hawkesbury. And the Canso Causeway, with Troy.

**EFFIE:** Is there mail in all of them?

**DEVELOPER:** No. The mail is only in Mabou. Everywhere else, you just drive. Which is why a certain grey car keeps turning up in other people's hedges.

**EFFIE:** What's the best thing outside Mabou?

**DEVELOPER:** In West Bay, open Places and choose "Haul to Sugar Camp." You're given a heavy pickup and a trailer. On the trailer are a side by side and a dirt bike, strapped down.

**EFFIE:** And I drive all that to the old quarry.

**DEVELOPER:** You haul it there, on real roads, and it tells you each turn. Then you unload. Ride the bike. Bring it back. Load it up again. It goes on in the right order, or it does not go on. I spent a week on the order.

**EFFIE:** A week. On the order things go on a trailer.

**DEVELOPER:** It is the most accurate trailer in Canadian fiction. Nobody asked me for it. That's how you know it's art.

**EFFIE:** Where does the game go next?

**DEVELOPER:** Wherever the road goes when it leaves the last place. I don't plan, Effie. I follow the centre line.

**EFFIE:** The developer, everybody. He's hauling himself out now.

## Part 13: More game tips (each one names a feature that exists)

### talk-tip-hill-height

**ANNOUNCER:** A Just Drive tip. The hills start at one and a half times their real height, for drama. Open Settings and find Hill height. Slide it down to one point zero for the true shape of the land. Or slide it up, and frighten yourself.

**ANNOUNCER:** A tip from the Party Line. The small map in the corner turns with your car, so the way ahead is always up. Tap the map to change how far it shows. Tap its name to fold it away. You'll still be lost. But tidily.
