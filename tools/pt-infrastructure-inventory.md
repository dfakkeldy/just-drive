A spillway usage tag describes an underground culvert, not a mapped open channel. Generic towers and boom-pier points have no size or light classification and are omitted. Small piers are six open line ways, not terminal polygons.

Reproduce with the project GDAL Python: `python tools/pt-infrastructure-extract.py data/work/pt-survey`, then `node tools/run-node-check.mjs tools/pt-infrastructure-ground.mjs data/work/pt-survey`. The sampler reads only encoded ground heights from the baked Point Tupper tiles. It samples conveyor and pipe routes at intervals no greater than 1 m, and other outlines at intervals no greater than 8 m. Supports remain about 8 m apart. Point gate posts use their own baked ground samples at each end. It omits a new culvert model when the centre of an existing baked culvert is within 20 m of its mapped line. Existing culvert mouths remain owned by streams.ts. All unspecified cross-sections and clearances are schematic defaults, listed in the source header. Point gates have a schematic east-west 4 m bar because the source has no orientation.

Road openings: the runtime tests each low part against the baked road network at 1 m intervals, with half the part width plus a 0.6 m margin. Parts that overlap a road are omitted. Galleries retain 5 m nominal clearance, but their supports leave road openings. Point gates on roads are omitted because their mapped points supply no direction; a point gate is drawn whole (both posts and the bar) or not at all, so one at a road edge leaves no lone post. This means a selected source can have no visible part. Dam cap triangles that intersect any road footprint are also omitted. Terrain stretch changes bases above sea level only; all structure dimensions and clearances stay in metres. Ground below sea level remains unstretched, like the terrain.

Road queries use a 32 m grid. Each road segment is stored in every cell touched by its box plus its half-width and the 0.6 m clearance margin. Queries collect all cells touched by the part or cap box, deduplicate segment ids, and run the exact tests. The grid changes candidate selection only. The full-road oracle still runs in the Node check. A before/after construction proof found byte-identical parts and cap vertices.
